### 2.1 Open the Phone app in Safe Mode

First and foremost, the thing that can let you get rid of this trouble is Safe Mode. It is a feature that will end any excessive background functioning of the Xiaomi 13T Pro device. For instance, your device will be able to run sans any third-party apps when in Safe mode. Since the important functions and the naïve apps will be running on the Xiaomi 13T Pro device, you will get to know if it’s really a software glitch or not by running the Phone app in Safe mode. And this is the first solution we would recommend you make use of when the Phone app has stopped. Here is how to enable Safe Mode.

1. Switch off the Xiaomi 13T Pro phone first.
2. Now keep pressing the "Power" button until you see the Xiaomi 13T Pro logo on the screen.
3. Release the button and immediately press and hold the "Volume Down" key.
4. Leave the key once the Xiaomi 13T Pro device is in Safe mode. Now, the third-party apps will be disabled and you can check if the Phone app is still not responding or everything is fine.

### 2.2 Clear the cache of the Phone app

The cache should be cleaned timely if you want any app to work properly. Due to the constant usage, the temporary files get collected and may corrupt if not cleared. Hence, the next solution you should try when the Phone app keeps stopping is to clear the cache. Here are the steps to be performed.

1. Open "Settings" on your device and go to "Application" or "Apps".
2. Now from the list of all applications, go to "Phone" and tap on it.
3. Now, click on "Storage" and choose "Clear Cache".

## What causes the Xiaomi 13T Pro Screen unresponsive?

The Xiaomi 13T Pro screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Xiaomi 13T Pro phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Xiaomi 13T Pro 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Xiaomi 13T Pro screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Xiaomi 13T Pro Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Xiaomi 13T Pro phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Xiaomi 13T Pro.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Xiaomi 13T Pro screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
