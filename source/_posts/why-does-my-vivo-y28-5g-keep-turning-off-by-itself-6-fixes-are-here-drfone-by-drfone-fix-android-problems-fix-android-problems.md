## Part 1: Possible reasons for phone turning off by itself

We understand your trouble when you ask, “Why does My Vivo Y28 5G keep turning off?” thus, here we have four of the possible reasons that might be causing the glitch and help you understand the problem better.

- **Step 1:** Open your phone's settings. Navigate to the **Accessibility** or **Vision** section.

![color inversion option turned on](https://images.wondershare.com/drfone/article/2023/11/phone-is-black-and-white-03.jpg)

- **Step 2:** Disable the **Grayscale** or **Color** **Inversion** option.
