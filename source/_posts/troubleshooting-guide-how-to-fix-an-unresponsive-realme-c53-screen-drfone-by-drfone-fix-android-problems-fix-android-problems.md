## What causes the Realme C53 Screen unresponsive?

The Realme C53 screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Realme C53 phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Realme C53 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Realme C53 screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Realme C53 Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Realme C53 phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Realme C53.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Realme C53 screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
