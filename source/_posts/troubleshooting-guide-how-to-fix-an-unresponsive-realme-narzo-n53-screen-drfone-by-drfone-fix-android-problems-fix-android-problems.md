This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Realme Narzo N53 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Realme Narzo N53 phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Realme Narzo N53.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Realme Narzo N53 screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
