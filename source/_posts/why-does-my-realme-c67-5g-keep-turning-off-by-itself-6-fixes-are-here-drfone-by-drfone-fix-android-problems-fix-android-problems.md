## Part 1: Possible reasons for phone turning off by itself

We understand your trouble when you ask, “Why does My Realme C67 5G keep turning off?” thus, here we have four of the possible reasons that might be causing the glitch and help you understand the problem better.

Then there are certain Applications that are not supported by the Android software. While using such Apps, the phone may turn off abruptly. This usually happens when you install Apps from unknown sources which are not compatible with Android.

Also, if your battery is low or has become too old, your phone may shut off and not work as smoothly.

## Common Causes of Realme C67 5G Screen Unresponsiveness

While Realme C67 5G devices offer reliable performance, users may encounter situations where the screen becomes unresponsive or stuck on boot. Soft-bricked software or corrupted firmware can cause an Realme C67 5G device to become unresponsive, including the screen stuck on the boot. This can happen to various models, such as the Realme C67 5G, A37, A5, and A5s. Software updates and resets can help fix these issues.

Over time, the touch screen of an Realme C67 5G device may become unresponsive due to wear and tear. This can happen to models like the Realme C67 5G F1s and F3. In this case, replacing the touch screen or seeking professional assistance may be necessary.

Dropping or impacting an Realme C67 5G device can cause physical damage to the touch screen, resulting in unresponsiveness. This can happen to various models like the Realme C67 5G Reno and Find X. In some cases, the damage may be repairable, but the screen replacement or the Realme C67 5G device itself may be necessary in severe cases. Protecting the Realme C67 5G device with a case and handling it carefully to prevent physical damage is important.
