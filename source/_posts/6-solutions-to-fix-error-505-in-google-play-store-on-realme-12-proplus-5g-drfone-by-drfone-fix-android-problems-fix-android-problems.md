## Part 4: Android Phone Won't Turn On: **Common Fix**

To try fixing an Android Phone that won’t turn on, follow these steps:

1. For any Android devices, remove the battery (considering the battery of your Realme 12 Pro+ 5G can be removed) and leave it out for at least 30 minutes. Put back the battery in and try to turn it on.
2. Press and hold down the **Power** and **Volume Down** buttons at the same time for 15-30 minutes to reboot the Realme 12 Pro+ 5G device.
