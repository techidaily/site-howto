## What causes the Tecno Pova 5 Screen unresponsive?

The Tecno Pova 5 screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Tecno Pova 5 phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Tecno Pova 5 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Tecno Pova 5 screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Tecno Pova 5 Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Tecno Pova 5 phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Tecno Pova 5.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Tecno Pova 5 screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.

### 2.3 Insert the SIM card correctly

If your SIM card was working properly until yet, there is a good possibility that the SIM card may have moved a bit from its actual location. Eventually, there is poor contact between the SIM card pins and the circuit. Try inserting your SIM card properly with the following steps.

- Turn your device back on and look if your SIM not provisioned on the Android issue is resolved or not.
