- **Inability to Enter Fastboot Mode.**If you find it increasingly challenging to access the fastboot mode, it could signify impending trouble.

Installing unofficial custom software, such as custom recoveries or third-party kernels, might not be compatible with the Infinix GT 10 Pro device's hardware or official software. That can cause conflicts that can lead to bricking.
