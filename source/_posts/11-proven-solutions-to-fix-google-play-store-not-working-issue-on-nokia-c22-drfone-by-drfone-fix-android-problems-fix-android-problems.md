## What causes the Nokia C22 Screen unresponsive?

The Nokia C22 screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Nokia C22 phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Nokia C22 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Nokia C22 screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Nokia C22 Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Nokia C22 phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Nokia C22.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Nokia C22 screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
