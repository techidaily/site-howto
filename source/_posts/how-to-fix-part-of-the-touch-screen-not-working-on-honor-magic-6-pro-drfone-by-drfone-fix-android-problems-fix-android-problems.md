### 2.1 Open the Phone app in Safe Mode

First and foremost, the thing that can let you get rid of this trouble is Safe Mode. It is a feature that will end any excessive background functioning of the Honor Magic 6 Pro device. 1. Switch off the Honor Magic 6 Pro phone first.

4. Leave the key once the Honor Magic 6 Pro device is in Safe mode. Now, the third-party apps will be disabled and you can check if the Phone app is still not responding or everything is fine.
