## What causes the Nokia G42 5G Screen unresponsive?

The Nokia G42 5G screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Nokia G42 5G phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Nokia G42 5G 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Nokia G42 5G screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Nokia G42 5G Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Nokia G42 5G phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Nokia G42 5G.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Nokia G42 5G screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
