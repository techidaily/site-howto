A boot loop describes a process when Your Xiaomi Redmi Note 12T Pro stays stuck in a circle of rebooting. When your phone is ****stuck on the startup screen****, one of the strategies you will try is to restart your device. When stuck in a boot loop, there are different troubleshooting methods for other devices. For Android phones, force your phone into its Recovery mode; from there, you can restore a previous backup. A boot loop typically starts when there is a change to your system before a reboot cycle.
