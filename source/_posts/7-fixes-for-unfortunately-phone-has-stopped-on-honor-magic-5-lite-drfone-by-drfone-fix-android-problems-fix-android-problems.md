### 2.1 Open the Phone app in Safe Mode

First and foremost, the thing that can let you get rid of this trouble is Safe Mode. It is a feature that will end any excessive background functioning of the Honor Magic 5 Lite device. For instance, your device will be able to run sans any third-party apps when in Safe mode. Since the important functions and the naïve apps will be running on the Honor Magic 5 Lite device, you will get to know if it’s really a software glitch or not by running the Phone app in Safe mode. And this is the first solution we would recommend you make use of when the Phone app has stopped. Here is how to enable Safe Mode.

1. Switch off the Honor Magic 5 Lite phone first.
2. Now keep pressing the "Power" button until you see the Honor Magic 5 Lite logo on the screen.
3. Release the button and immediately press and hold the "Volume Down" key.
4. Leave the key once the Honor Magic 5 Lite device is in Safe mode. Now, the third-party apps will be disabled and you can check if the Phone app is still not responding or everything is fine.

3. Now, click on "Storage" and choose "Clear Cache".
