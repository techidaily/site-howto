If you’re unable to connect to the internet, but other devices have no issues, you may find the issues is coming from within your Motorola Razr 40 itself. Fortunately, there are ways you can see whether this is the problem.

The best way to do this is to boot your device in Safe Mode. The process for doing this will depend on the make and model of your Motorola Razr 40, but the basic process goes as follows;

1. Turn off your Motorola Razr 40 by holding down the Power button and tapping Power Off. Wait a few minutes to ensure the Motorola Razr 40 device is completely off
2. Press and hold the Power button to turn your phone on, but the press and hold both the volume up and volume down buttons at the same time
3. You will see the words ‘Safe Mode’ appear on your screen as the Motorola Razr 40 device loads
4. Now you will be booted in Safe Mode. Try connecting to the internet again to see if it works

If your device connects to the internet while in Safe Mode, you’ll know you have a problem with an app or service that’s running on your device. If this is the case, you’ll need to go through your apps and delete them and then reinstall them one at a time until you find the app or service that is causing your internet problems.
