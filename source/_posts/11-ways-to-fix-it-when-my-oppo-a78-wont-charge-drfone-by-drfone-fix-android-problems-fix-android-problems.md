In some cases, the damage may be repairable, but the screen replacement or the Oppo A78 device itself may be necessary in severe cases. Protecting the Oppo A78 device with a case and handling it carefully to prevent physical damage is important.

While Oppo A78 devices are generally reliable, various factors can contribute to screen unresponsiveness. Users can effectively troubleshoot and resolve issues by identifying these potential causes and seeking appropriate solutions.
