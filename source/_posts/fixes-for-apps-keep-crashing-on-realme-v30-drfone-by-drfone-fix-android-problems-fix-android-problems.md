- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Realme V30 screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Realme V30 Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Realme V30 phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Realme V30.
