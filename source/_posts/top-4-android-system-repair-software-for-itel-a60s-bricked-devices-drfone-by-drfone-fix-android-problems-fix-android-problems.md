### Common Causes of a Brick Itel A60s

Something might go wrong during the process when you're trying to update your Itel A60s device. This is properly because of a poor internet connection or other interruptions. As a result, your device might get confused and end up bricked.

- **Custom ROM Installations**

A custom ROM is like a customized version of your device's software. While it can bring cool new features, it can also cause issues if not installed correctly. Incompatible or poorly made custom ROMs can sometimes lead to a bricked device.

If you're trying to tweak your device's software without proper knowledge, you might make changes that the Itel A60s device can't handle. This can lead to instability and, you guessed it, a bricked device.
