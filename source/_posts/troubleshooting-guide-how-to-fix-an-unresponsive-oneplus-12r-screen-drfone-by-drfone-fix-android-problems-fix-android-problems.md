## What causes the OnePlus 12R Screen unresponsive?

The OnePlus 12R screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a OnePlus 12R phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the OnePlus 12R 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the OnePlus 12R screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some OnePlus 12R Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a OnePlus 12R phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the OnePlus 12R.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of OnePlus 12R screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
