## Part 6. Check SSID and IP address on Android

To make a Wi-Fi connection work, your Motorola Edge 2023 needs to match two codes that connect and relate to your router to establish the connection and work correctly. These are known as the SSID and the IP address.

Every wireless device will have its own codes and making sure they’re matched to the network you’re connecting to is crucial to ensuring the internet is working on your Motorola Edge 2023. Here’s how to check on your Motorola Edge 2023 to make sure everything is correct.

1. Tap the Settings menu option, followed by Wi-Fi on your Motorola Edge 2023
2. Turn on your Wi-Fi network and connect it to your router
3. Find the router name (SSID) and make sure it’s exactly the same as the SSID written on your router
4. Once connected, tap the Wi-Fi network and you will see the IP address. Check both your phone and the router codes to make sure this number matches
