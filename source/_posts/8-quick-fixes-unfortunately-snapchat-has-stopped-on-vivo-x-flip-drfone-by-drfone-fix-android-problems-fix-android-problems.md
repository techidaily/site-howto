Every wireless device will have its own codes and making sure they’re matched to the network you’re connecting to is crucial to ensuring the internet is working on your Vivo X Flip. Here’s how to check on your Vivo X Flip to make sure everything is correct.

1. Tap the Settings menu option, followed by Wi-Fi on your Vivo X Flip
2. Turn on your Wi-Fi network and connect it to your router

When these numbers are matching, if your Android is still not connecting to wifi, you’ll know this wasn’t the problem.

7. **Overclocking or Modifications:** Rooting or modifying the Android device, or applying overclocking settings, can lead to instability and the process system not responding error.
