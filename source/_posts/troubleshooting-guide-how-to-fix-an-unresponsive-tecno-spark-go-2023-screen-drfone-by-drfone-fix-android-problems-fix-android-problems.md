## What causes the Tecno Spark Go (2023) Screen unresponsive?

The Tecno Spark Go (2023) screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Tecno Spark Go (2023) phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Tecno Spark Go (2023) 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Tecno Spark Go (2023) screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Tecno Spark Go (2023) Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Tecno Spark Go (2023) phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Tecno Spark Go (2023).

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Tecno Spark Go (2023) screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
