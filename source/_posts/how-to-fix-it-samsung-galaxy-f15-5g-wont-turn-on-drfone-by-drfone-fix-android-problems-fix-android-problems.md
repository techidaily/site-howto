## Part 4: Android Phone Won't Turn On: **Common Fix**

To try fixing an Android Phone that won’t turn on, follow these steps:

1. For any Android devices, remove the battery (considering the battery of your Samsung Galaxy F15 5G can be removed) and leave it out for at least 30 minutes. Put back the battery in and try to turn it on.
2. Press and hold down the **Power** and **Volume Down** buttons at the same time for 15-30 minutes to reboot the Samsung Galaxy F15 5G device.
3. Should the first two steps do not work, charge your Samsung Galaxy F15 5G to get it out of the start-up loop. You can also opt to use a different battery, just in case your current battery is the source of the problem.
4. If there are any connected hardware e.g. SD card, remove them from the Samsung Galaxy F15 5G device.
5. Start your Samsung Galaxy F15 5G in **Safe Mode** by pressing and holding down the **Menu** or **Volume Down** button on your device.
6. Should the first five steps do not work for you, perform a hard reset. Do take note that each device will have a different way to do so and that data that is stored locally on the phone will be deleted.
7. Send your Samsung Galaxy F15 5G to the repair shop should none of these steps work.

Over time, the touch screen of an Samsung Galaxy F15 5G device may become unresponsive due to wear and tear. This can happen to models like the Samsung Galaxy F15 5G F1s and F3. In this case, replacing the touch screen or seeking professional assistance may be necessary.

This can happen to various models like the Samsung Galaxy F15 5G Reno and Find X. In some cases, the damage may be repairable, but the screen replacement or the Samsung Galaxy F15 5G device itself may be necessary in severe cases. Protecting the Samsung Galaxy F15 5G device with a case and handling it carefully to prevent physical damage is important.

While Samsung Galaxy F15 5G devices are generally reliable, various factors can contribute to screen unresponsiveness. Users can effectively troubleshoot and resolve issues by identifying these potential causes and seeking appropriate solutions.
