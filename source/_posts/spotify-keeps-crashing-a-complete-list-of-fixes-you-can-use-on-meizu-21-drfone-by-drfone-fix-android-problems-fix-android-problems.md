## What causes the Meizu 21 Screen unresponsive?

The Meizu 21 screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Meizu 21 phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Meizu 21 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Meizu 21 screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Meizu 21 Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Meizu 21 phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Meizu 21.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Meizu 21 screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
