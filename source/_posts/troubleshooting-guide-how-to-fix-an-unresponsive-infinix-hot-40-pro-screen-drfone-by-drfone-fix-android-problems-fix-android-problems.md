## What causes the Infinix Hot 40 Pro Screen unresponsive?

The Infinix Hot 40 Pro screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Infinix Hot 40 Pro phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Infinix Hot 40 Pro 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Infinix Hot 40 Pro screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Infinix Hot 40 Pro Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Infinix Hot 40 Pro phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Infinix Hot 40 Pro.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Infinix Hot 40 Pro screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
