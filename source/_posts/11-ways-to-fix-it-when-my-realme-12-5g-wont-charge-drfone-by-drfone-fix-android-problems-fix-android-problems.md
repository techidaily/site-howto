## What causes the Realme 12 5G Screen unresponsive?

The Realme 12 5G screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Realme 12 5G phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Realme 12 5G 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Realme 12 5G screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Realme 12 5G Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Realme 12 5G phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Realme 12 5G.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Realme 12 5G screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
