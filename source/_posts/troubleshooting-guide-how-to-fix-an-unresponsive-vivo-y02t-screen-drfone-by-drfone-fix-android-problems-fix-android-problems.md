## What causes the Vivo Y02T Screen unresponsive?

The Vivo Y02T screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **During Use/Gaming:** The screen may become unresponsive due to overheating when using or gaming on a Vivo Y02T phone. This can be caused by running too many apps simultaneously or playing games for an extended period, especially on models such as the Vivo Y02T 9 Pro, which generates much heat during gaming.

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Vivo Y02T screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Vivo Y02T Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Vivo Y02T phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Vivo Y02T.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Vivo Y02T screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.

Step 3: Go to Local> Device> efs> Factory App and then open factorymode as text in the “ES Note Editor” Turn it ON
