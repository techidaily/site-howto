## Part 1. What is Android Factory Mode?

Factory mode or what is commonly known as factory reset is one of the options available for you when your Vivo V30 Lite 5G is in recovery mode. Several options are available for you once you enter Recovery mode on your device but few are as effective as the wipe data/ factory reset option. If you have been using your Vivo V30 Lite 5G for a while now and its performance to be less than ideal, a factory reset might be a good solution. It is however important to note that a factory reset or factory mode does often result in the loss of all your data. Therefore a backup is necessary to protect against this data loss risk.

Step 2: Next, go to “Tools” and then turn on “Root Explorer”

Step 3: Go to Local> Device> efs> Factory App and then open factorymode as text in the “ES Note Editor” Turn it ON
