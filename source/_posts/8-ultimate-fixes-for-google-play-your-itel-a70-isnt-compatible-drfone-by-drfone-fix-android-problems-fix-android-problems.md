The Itel A70 screen may become unresponsive due to various reasons. Here are some common situations and their possible causes:

- **No Response When Touching the Screen:**Sometimes, the Itel A70 screen may not respond when touched due to a faulty touch screen or display. This can happen due to physical damage or a manufacturing defect. Some Itel A70 Nord 2 users have reported this issue.

- **After Dropping the Phone:** If a Itel A70 phone has been dropped, the screen may become unresponsive due to physical damage or a loose connection. This can happen to any model, including the Itel A70.

Overheating, physical damage, and manufacturing defects are some common causes of Itel A70 screen unresponsiveness, which may occur during use/gaming, no response when touching the screen, or after dropping the phone.
