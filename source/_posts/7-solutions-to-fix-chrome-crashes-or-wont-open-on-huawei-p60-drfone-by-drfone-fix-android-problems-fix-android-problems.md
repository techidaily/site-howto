## Part 1: Understanding Bricked Huawei P60

Have you ever heard the term "bricked" concerning your Huawei P60 device? This section explains it to you in simple terms. Imagine your device becoming like a brick, unresponsive and seemingly lifeless. That's what happens when a **Huawei P60 gets bricked**. There are two main types of brick: soft brick and hard brick.

### Soft Brick

Soft brick is like a temporary setback for your device. To see if your **Huawei P60 is soft - bricked**, check out the details below:

- It happens when the software encounters issues, but the hardware is usually fine.
- Your device might not start properly, [<u>freeze</u>](https://drfone.wondershare.com/iphone-problems/fix-iphone-freezing-after-updating-to-ios-17.html), or [<u>get stuck on the logo screen</u>](https://drfone.wondershare.com/iphone-recovery/iphone-stuck-at-the-apple-logo.html).

### Hard Brick

Now, this is a more severe situation. In this case, your **bricked Huawei P60** might fall under the following conditions:

- Thesoftware and hardware have significant problems, making the Huawei P60 device unresponsive.
- Your device won't turn on and might not show any signs of life.

![bricked Huawei P60](https://images.wondershare.com/drfone/article/2023/08/system-repair-software-for-samsung-bricked-02.jpg)

### Common Causes of a Brick Huawei P60

Now, let's discuss why devices end up in this bricked state. It can happen due to a few common reasons:

- **Failed Updates**

Something might go wrong during the process when you're trying to update your Huawei P60 device. This is properly because of a poor internet connection or other interruptions. As a result, your device might get confused and end up bricked.

- **Custom ROM Installations**

A custom ROM is like a customized version of your device's software. While it can bring cool new features, it can also cause issues if not installed correctly. Incompatible or poorly made custom ROMs can sometimes lead to a bricked device.

- **Unauthorized Modifications**

If you're trying to tweak your device's software without proper knowledge, you might make changes that the Huawei P60 device can't handle. This can lead to instability and, you guessed it, a bricked device.

So, why is it important to know all this? Well, a bricked device isn't just frustrating; it can disrupt your daily life. You won't be able to use your device, access your apps, or even make calls. That's why unbricking is crucial.
