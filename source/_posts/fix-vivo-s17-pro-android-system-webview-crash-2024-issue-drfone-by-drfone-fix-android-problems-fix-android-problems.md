Android WebView is an integral component of Android apps, acting as the invisible engine that renders web content within these apps. - **App Freezing**

One of the earliest signs of an **Vivo S17 Pro Android System WebView crash** is the app freezing. The app may become unresponsive, refusing to respond to your taps or gestures.

Vivo S17 Pro Android System WebView requires a certain amount of memory to operate properly. If your device is running low on memory, it can cause Vivo S17 Pro Android System WebView to crash. It is more likely to happen on older devices with less RAM.

WebView might crash if it conflicts with other resources or processes running on the Vivo S17 Pro device. It can happen if the Vivo S17 Pro device runs low on memory or other resource-intensive apps compete for system resources.
