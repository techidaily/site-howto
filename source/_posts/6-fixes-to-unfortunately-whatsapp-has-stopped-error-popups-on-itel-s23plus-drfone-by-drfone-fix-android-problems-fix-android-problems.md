## Part 3. Healthy Habits To Consider To Prolong Your One Plus

Maintaining the longevity of your Itel S23+ device requires adopting proactive habits. Here are vital tips to consider.

- **Battery Care.**Avoid frequent deep discharges and overcharging. Use original charging accessories and refrain from exposing the Itel S23+ device to extreme temperatures.
- **App Management.**Review and uninstall apps you no longer use. This reduces clutter and conserves system resources, promoting smoother device operation.
