### Reason 1. Physical Impact or Dropping

**Solution:** If the Sony Xperia 5 V 3 has experienced a significant impact or drop, inspect the exterior for any visible damage. A cracked screen or internal hardware issue might prevent the Sony Xperia 5 V device from turning on. Consider professional repair services if necessary.
