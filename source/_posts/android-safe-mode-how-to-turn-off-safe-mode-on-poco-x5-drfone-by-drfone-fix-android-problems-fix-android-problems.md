## Part 1: How to turn off safe mode on Android?

This is the easiest method to turn off the safe mode in Android. To use this method, follow the steps below.

Step 1 – Tap and hold the power button of your Poco X5.

Step 2 – You could find the option “Restart”. Tap on it. (If you have only one option, move to the step no 2)

Step 3 – Now, your phone will boot up in some time and you can see the Poco X5 device is no more on safe mode.

Your Google Play Balance is linked to the country setting on your Google Play account. Changing countries on your account results in the inability to access or utilize the existing balance.
